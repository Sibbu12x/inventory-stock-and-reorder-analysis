HOW THE SIGNAL WORKS

A simple, explainable reorder rule

The same calculation is applied to every product at each location.

REORDER POINT⌈ Daily demand × lead time ⌉ + safety stock
AVAILABLE STOCKOn hand − reserved + incoming
ORDER QUANTITYMax(shortfall, supplier minimum)
i

Order quantity is zero when available stock meets or exceeds the reorder point. Supplier minimums may make the proposed order larger than the immediate shortfall.
